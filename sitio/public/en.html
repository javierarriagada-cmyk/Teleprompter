How it works

What it’s for

For your videos, classes, talks and presentations.

Read your script at your own pace while recording a video, preparing a class or rehearsing a presentation.

Videos

Record with the front or rear camera, in portrait or landscape. See yourself behind the text, in a movable window, or keep the camera view hidden while recording.

Classes

Prepare your class script and read at your own pace. When you pause, the text waits.

Presentations

Rehearse your presentation without recording. Read at your own pace as the text follows your voice.

Rehearsals

You can also read without recording, at a fixed speed, until the words feel natural.

The app itself

Read, record and export.

Adjust text size, font, width and colors. Add bold text, colors and highlights to your script.

Your scripts, with you.
