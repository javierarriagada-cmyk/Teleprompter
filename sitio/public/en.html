The teleprompter that follows your voice.

Read

You talk. It follows.

Speak, and the text moves with you. No hands, no scrolling.

It waits

Others keep scrolling. Sigo waits.

Stop to think, and the text waits for you. Start talking again, and it picks up right where you are.

Your script

Mark what matters.

Bold the lines you want to land. Color the shifts in tone. Highlight what you can’t afford to miss.

Record

Eyes on the lens.

The text sits right under the camera. You look into the lens, not down at the screen. Pause whenever you want: what happens during the pause stays out of the video. Away from the phone, your palm pauses or resumes, and while paused a V sign finishes. It starts off, and only works with the front camera.

Your video

Captions that keep time.

When you’re done, your video comes with captions built from your script, in step with your voice. Pick a filter while you watch it: Natural, Warm, Cool, Vivid, Soft or black and white. What you see is what gets saved. Save it to your gallery or share it, portrait, landscape or on a tablet.

Offline

No signal? No problem.

Works without internet. Your voice never leaves your phone.

A regular teleprompter
Today I want to tell you something important. In a short video or a long one, reading off a sheet of paper always shows. Now I read, I talk, and the text follows me. No rushing. No losing my place. And whatever I can't afford to miss, I mark in yellow.
If I stop to think, the text waits for me. When I start talking again, it keeps up with me. I look at the lens, not at the screen, and whoever is watching feels I'm talking straight to them.
If I pause, that stretch stays out. When I'm done the video already has its captions. I share it just as it came out.
