# Google Play screenshot design references

Viewed 12 Nov 2026 on the public Google Play web listing, US English region; no sign-in or install/purchase actions. Listing screenshots are 1920×1072 page captures with the screenshot strip visible. The `*-full-01..03.png` files are the actual first three public Play screenshot assets, converted to RGB PNG at the largest public size served (PromptSmart’s source assets are smaller). I clicked each of the first three screenshot thumbnails in Play; the web viewer opened its dark overlay/arrows but did not render the enlarged image in the browser capture, so the full files preserve the exact store image content without viewer chrome. `*-graphic.png` files are crops of the visible top hero media area from the listing page, not downloads of a separate source feature-graphic asset.

## 1. BIGVU
- **App:** BIGVU Teleprompter & Captions · **Developer:** BIGVU AI Eye Contact & Video Subtitles · **Package:** `bigvu.com.reporter`
- **Play:** https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=bigvu.com.reporter
- **First six, in store order:** 1) “the best teleprompter,” with a phone and a person reading; 2) “beauty filters & effects,” phone/photo effects; 3) “eye-contact fix with AI,” before/after eyes; 4) “subtitles & auto b-roll,” video and subtitle example; 5) “smart clipping best moments,” multiple social video cuts; 6) “text to video in seconds,” script-to-video example.
- **Format / text / UI:** First six are portrait 9:16 phone-format art (the first three files are 1079×1920). First three use a large two-line headline, plus short on-screen copy; #1 also adds a “12M users” badge. Phones are framed in #1/#2/#4/#6; #3 enlarges the before/after eye result. This is a designed promo composition, not a plain full-screen app capture.
- **First-three promise:** read to camera with a scrolling prompt; beauty effects; AI eye-contact correction.
- **Copyable techniques:** oversized navy type on white; one clear benefit headline; let an enlarged phone/result carry the proof, with a restrained blue accent.
- **Top media:** the top hero is a trailer/video-style panel with artwork and people; a small “Trailer” play control sits at the lower right. No play icon covers the center in the captured state. Saved as `bigvu-graphic.png`.
